## 1. The one-liner

**Meeting Agent is an AI assistant that joins your Zoom and Google Meet calls, transcribes everything in real time, and delivers a clean summary with action items — in English, Bahasa Malaysia, Chinese, and Iban, with your data kept in Malaysia.**

It is the first AI meeting assistant built for the Malaysian market, not a foreign tool forced to fit it.

---

## 2. The problem

Every organisation runs on meetings, and meetings leak value:

- People half-listen because they are busy taking notes.
- Decisions and action items get lost the moment the call ends.
- Minutes are written by hand, hours later, often inaccurately.
- Multilingual teams (very common in Sarawak) mix languages in one meeting, and no foreign tool handles that well.
- Government bodies and GLCs cannot use foreign cloud tools at all, because their data is not allowed to leave the country.

The global tools (Otter, Fireflies, Fathom) solve the first three problems for English-only, cloud-only, credit-card customers. They do not solve the Malaysian reality.

---

## 3. The product (what it does)

1. **You paste a meeting link.** Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams.
2. **An AI agent joins the call** as a participant and records + transcribes live.
3. **It writes the notes for you** — a summary, key highlights, and a checklist of action items with owners.
4. **Everything is searchable and exportable**, organised by meeting, in your language.

Built-in from day one:
- Real-time transcription with speaker identification.
- AI summaries and auto-extracted action items.
- **Four languages: English, Bahasa Malaysia, Chinese, and Iban.**
- Dashboard, meeting history, user profiles, subscriptions, admin panel.
- PDF / text export for records and claims.

## 5. The unfair advantage (our moat)

1. **Language.** We speak Bahasa Malaysia and **Iban** — no global competitor markets in BM, and none support Iban at all. This is a genuine, defensible local moat.
2. **Data sovereignty.** We can deploy on a customer's own server (on-premise / private cloud). Government and GLC data is legally required to stay in-country. Otter and Fireflies *cannot* offer this. It is the key that unlocks the most valuable buyers.
3. **Local commercial fit.** We bill the way Malaysian businesses actually pay — bank transfer, FPX/DuitNow, purchase orders, LHDN-compliant invoices — not just credit cards.
4. **Grant alignment.** The product is positioned to be claimable under SME digitalisation grants, which removes the price objection entirely for small businesses.

## 7. Business model & pricing

A simple ladder that scales from individual to enterprise:

| Tier | Price | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | RM 0 — 3 meetings/month | Trial, lead generation |
| **Pro** | RM 59/month (or RM 590/year) | Individuals, consultants |
| **Team** | RM 49/seat/month (min 5 seats) | SMEs, firms, departments |
| **Pay-per-use** | RM 12/meeting | Occasional users |
| **Enterprise / White-label** | Custom, from ~RM 30k/year | Government, GLC, corporates |

The revenue engine is **Team (per-seat)** and **Enterprise (white-label & on-premise)**. Individual subscriptions build the brand and the funnel; corporate contracts pay the bills.

---

## 8. The corporate & white-label play (where the money is)

For organisations that want Meeting Agent as their own in-house software, three models:

1. **Team / volume licence** — per-seat, annual contract, single sign-on, onboarding and training. Indicative RM 30k–80k/year for 50–200 staff.
2. **White-label** — we host it under *their* brand and domain (e.g. `meetings.theircompany.com.my`). Setup fee from RM 25k plus an annual licence. Ideal for agencies and resellers.
3. **On-premise / private cloud** — deployed on the customer's own servers; their data never leaves their walls. Setup from RM 40k plus an annual support retainer. This is the offering for government and GLCs.

**Corporate sales flow:** demo request → discovery call → 30-day pilot in one department → quotation / purchase order → deployment + training → ongoing support retainer.

*(Illustrative, to show the shape of the business — not audited figures.)*
