# Meeting Agent — Commercial Strategy (Sarawak / Malaysia)

> Internal strategy doc. Market: Sarawak & Malaysia. Goal: raise ARPU, add corporate/
> white-label sales motion, protect margins. Currency: RM (MYR). Prices ex-SST unless noted.

---

## 1. Diagnosis — where current pricing leaks money

| Current | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| RM 50/mo "unlimited" | Every meeting costs real money (Vexa compute + Claude). Heavy users = loss. Also underpriced vs Otter (~RM 80) / Fireflies (~RM 85). | Move to **fair-use hours**, raise to RM 59. |
| Annual RM 300 (50% off RM 600) | Discount far too deep — halves ARPU, trains buyers to never pay monthly. | **2 months free (~17%)**, the SaaS standard. |
| No team / corporate tier | Biggest gap. B2B per-seat + govt/GLC is where Malaysian money is. | Add **Team (per-seat)** + **Enterprise**. |
| No data-sovereignty / white-label offer | Sarawak govt & GLCs need data in-country. You can self-host (Vexa). | Lead govt pitches with **on-prem**. |

**Where the uplift actually comes from** (not from charging individuals more — Malaysia is price-sensitive at consumer level):
1. Kill the 50% annual discount.
2. "Unlimited" → hours-based (protects margin).
3. **Per-seat Team tier** — multiplies ARPU 5–20×.
4. **Enterprise / white-label** — highest margin, annual contracts.

## 2. New public pricing ladder

| Tier | Price | Target | Key mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | RM 0 — 3 meetings/mo | Lead-gen / trial | No card. Pure hook. |
| **Pro** | **RM 59/mo** or **RM 590/yr** (2 mo free) | Individuals, consultants | Fair-use ~30 hrs/mo (not "unlimited"). |
| **Team** ⭐ | **RM 49/seat/mo**, min 5 seats, billed annually | SMEs, firms, departments | Admin console, shared workspace, central billing. **Revenue engine.** |
| **Pay-per-use** | **RM 12/meeting** | Occasional users | No commitment, top-up. |
| **Enterprise / White-label** | **"Contact us"** (from ~RM 30k/yr) | Govt, GLC, corporates | Custom — see §3. |

Pricing-psychology notes:
- 3 public paid tiers + "Contact us" = classic good/better/best with an anchor. Team is the visually highlighted "most popular."
- Charm pricing (RM 59, not RM 60) for consumer tiers; round numbers for enterprise (signals premium).
- Annual shown as "2 months free" framing beats "% off" for retention.

## 3. Corporate onboarding + White-label (highest margin)

Three "Contact sales" models:

1. **Team / Volume license** — per-seat, annual contract, SSO, onboarding session. ~RM 30k–80k/yr for 50–200 staff.
2. **White-label SaaS** — you host, *their* brand + domain (`meetings.theircompany.com.my`). **Setup from RM 25k** + annual license + per-seat. For agencies/resellers.
3. **On-premise / private cloud** ⭐ — deployed on *their* server, data never leaves their walls. **Setup from RM 40k** + annual maintenance retainer. **The government/GLC wedge** — PDPA + data residency is non-negotiable; Otter/Fireflies cannot offer it.

**Corporate sales flow:** Demo request → discovery call → 30-day pilot (1 dept) → quotation/LPO → deploy + training → support retainer. Build for **PO / bank transfer**, not credit cards.

---

## 4. Malaysia / Sarawak levers (these close deals)

- **SME Digitalisation Grant** — Malaysian SMEs claim matching grants (up to RM 5,000) for digital tools. Become a registered solution provider → market as **"grant-claimable"** → erases price objection. Highest-leverage move.
- **Data sovereignty** — lead every govt/GLC pitch with "your data stays in Malaysia / on your servers." The one thing global competitors can't say.
- **MyInvois / LHDN e-invoicing** — mandatory phased rollout. Make **LHDN-compliant e-invoices** a built-in feature + selling point.
- **SST 8%** — B2C prices "SST included," B2B "exclusive of SST." Issue proper tax invoices.
- **Payment rails** — **FPX / DuitNow / bank transfer + corporate PO**. Most Malaysian B2B won't pay by card.
- **Procurement access** — register on **ePerolehan** (federal) + Sarawak state vendor lists for tender/quotation eligibility.

## 5. Positioning for Sarawak (the unfair advantage)

Already supports **English, BM, Chinese, Iban** — no global competitor speaks Iban or markets in BM.

> "The only AI meeting assistant built for Malaysia — speaks Bahasa Malaysia, Iban,
> Chinese & English, and keeps your data in-country."

**Target buyers:** state agencies (via SDEC), GLCs (Sarawak Energy, Petros), law/accounting firms, universities (UNIMAS, Curtin, Swinburne Sarawak), chambers (SCCCI).
**Channels:** partner with SDEC + the digitalisation grant program; chambers of commerce; pilot-led referrals.

## 6. Rollout order (fastest money first)

1. Kill 50% annual discount → "2 months free."  *(tiny change, immediate ARPU lift)*
2. Add **Team per-seat tier** to the site.  *(opens B2B)*
3. Add **Enterprise / white-label section** + **Request-a-demo form**.  *(opens govt/GLC)*
4. Register for SME grant provider status + ePerolehan.  *(removes objections, unlocks tenders)*
5. Build LHDN e-invoice + FPX/DuitNow billing.  *(B2B requirement)*
